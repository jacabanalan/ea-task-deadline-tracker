EA Task & Deadline Tracker

A sample task and deadline tracker designed for Executive and Administrative Assistant work.

Purpose

The goal of this tracker is to keep tasks organized, identify priorities, and make sure important deadlines and follow-ups are not missed.

What It Tracks
Task
Executive / Team
Priority
Due Date
Status
Next Action
Follow-Up Date
Notes
Example Workflow
Receive a request from an executive or team member.
Add the task to the tracker.
Assign a priority and due date.
Identify the next action.
Follow up when needed.
Update the status.
Confirm completion.
Priority Levels

High – Urgent or deadline-sensitive

Medium – Important but not immediately urgent

Low – Can be completed as time allows
